How to use Luna X
Quick guide for each section
Create & Schedule Posts
The main posting workflow — voice or text to published content in seconds
1
Upload your media — drag a video or photo into the upload area, or tap the camera icon to select from your device. Luna X supports MP4, MOV, JPG, and PNG.
2
Give a command — type or speak what you want. Try: "Post this landscaping job today at 2pm on Instagram and Facebook with a great caption" or simply "Schedule for tomorrow morning." The AI handles the caption.
3
Review mode — if Review mode is ON, you'll see the generated caption and scheduled time before it saves. Edit anything you want, then confirm.
4
Auto-post mode — toggle Auto-post ON to skip the review step. Luna X will generate the caption and schedule it automatically after you speak your command.
5
Voice commands — tap the microphone and speak naturally. Recording stops automatically. Works great for hands-free posting from a job site.
💡 Pro tip: Mention the location in your command — "this Denver landscaping job" — and the AI will weave it into the caption naturally.
Posted Content
View, manage, and repost all your scheduled and published content
1
Filter by status — use the tabs at the top (All, Posted, Scheduled, Drafts, Instagram, Facebook) to filter your content by platform or status.
2
Scheduled posts — posts with a future time show as "scheduled" and will automatically publish when the time arrives. Luna X checks every minute.
3
Repost — click Repost on any published post to quickly schedule it again. Great for evergreen content like before/after shots.
4
Failed posts — if a post fails, you'll see a red error message on the card explaining why. Fix the issue and use Repost to try again.
5
Duplicate — use Duplicate to create a copy of any post and edit it before scheduling again.
💡 Pro tip: Check this page after your scheduled post time to confirm it published. You'll see it move from "scheduled" to "posted."
Analytics
Track your posting streak, volume, and consistency over time
1
Posting streak — tracks how many consecutive days you've posted. Consistency is the #1 driver of organic growth on Instagram and Facebook.
2
Posts & reach chart — shows your posting volume over time. Use this to identify gaps and stay consistent.
3
Platform breakdown — see how many posts went to Instagram vs Facebook so you can balance your distribution.
4
Top performing posts — your most recent published content ranked by engagement. Use these to identify what content resonates with your audience.
💡 Pro tip: Aim for at least 4 posts per week to build a posting streak. Use the calendar view to plan ahead.
Content Calendar
Visual overview of your posting schedule
1
See your schedule — the calendar shows all scheduled and published posts by day. Green dots = published, purple = scheduled.
2
Click a day — click any day to see posts scheduled for that date and their status.
3
Identify gaps — empty days show where you need more content. Use this to plan your recording sessions.
4
Navigate months — use the arrow buttons to move between months and plan weeks or months ahead.
💡 Pro tip: Batch record 5–10 videos at one job site, then schedule them spread across 2 weeks. Consistent presence without daily effort.
Meta Ads Dashboard
View campaign performance and get AI-powered optimization advice
1
Enter your ad account ID — find it in Meta Business Manager. Format: act_XXXXXXXXXX. Paste it in the field at the top and click Sync.
2
KPI cards — the top row shows total spend, reach, impressions, clicks, CTR, and CPC for your selected date range. Green = performing well, red = needs attention.
3
AI insights — the purple bar at the top gives you 2–3 specific recommendations based on your live data. Click Refresh to regenerate.
4
Tell AI what you're seeing — describe a problem in plain English ("leads aren't booking" or "CPL is too high") and click Analyze for a detailed diagnosis and fix plan.
5
Campaigns / Ad sets / Creatives tabs — drill into specific campaigns, ad sets, and individual ads to see granular performance data.
6
Insights chart — visualize spend, reach, or CTR over time. Use the dropdown to switch metrics.
💡 Pro tip: Check the Insights chart tab to see which days your ads perform best. Schedule your posts on those days to amplify organic + paid reach.
Settings
Configure your accounts, brand voice, and notification preferences
1
Connect Meta — go to Settings → Accounts tab → Connect Facebook. This links your Facebook Pages and Instagram Business accounts so Luna X can post on your behalf.
2
Select your page — after connecting, choose which Facebook Page and Instagram account to post to. Luna X remembers this across sessions.
3
Brand Voice — go to the Brand tab and fill in your business name, location, tone, and default hashtags. The AI uses these to write captions that sound like you.
4
Location — add your city/neighborhood in Brand settings. Luna X will naturally mention it in captions (e.g., "serving Denver homeowners").
5
Notifications — configure which email alerts you want: post published, post failed, weekly summary. All go to your account email.
💡 Pro tip: Fill in your Brand Voice settings first before posting. Better brand context = better AI captions from day one.
